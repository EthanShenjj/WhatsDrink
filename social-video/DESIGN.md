# WhatsDrink Social Video — Velvet Brew

## Style Prompt

Create a warm, tactile, premium vertical product film that feels like a quiet coffee ritual. Use generous negative space, paper-like cream surfaces, ultra-rounded white cards, cloud-soft brown shadows, and one lively terracotta accent. The mood is gentle, playful, and reassuring rather than loud or salesy. Motion should feel fluid and touchable: soft pushes, restrained overshoot, slow breathing decor, and a calm blur transition into the final brand frame.

## Colors

- Soft Cream — `#FCF9F4` — global canvas
- Warm Brown — `#321F13` — primary text and buttons
- Coffee Brown — `#4A3427` — structural UI and deep accents
- Terracotta — `#C96F4B` — primary social-video accent
- Warm White — `#FFFFFF` — elevated cards
- Supporting wheel colors — `#D08A6F`, `#E6BD72`, `#94A884`, `#7895AF`, `#B88C73`

## Typography

- Latin display and labels: `Manrope`, weights 700–900
- Chinese display and body: `PingFang SC`, weights 300–800
- Latin body fallback: `Be Vietnam Pro`
- Headlines are 76–132px with tight tracking; supporting copy is at least 30px.

## Motion

- Entrances feel responsive and soft, using `expo.out`, `back.out`, `power3.out`, and `sine.out`.
- Primary scene changes are directional pushes, matching the “make a choice, move forward” narrative.
- The final transition is a calm blur crossfade.
- Ambient motion is slow and finite: gentle floating, breathing scale, and restrained wheel rotation.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use neon colors, cold blue gradients, black backgrounds, or hard metallic effects.
- Do not use square cards, harsh borders, or crisp drop shadows.
- Do not fill the frame with dense UI or tiny web-sized text.
- Do not use generic tech iconography, fake analytics, or exaggerated health claims.
- Do not use abrupt jump cuts or aggressive glitch transitions.
