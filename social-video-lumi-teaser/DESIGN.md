# 拾光迹 Lumi 3D Teaser — Memory Portal

## Style Prompt

Create a premium vertical 3D character teaser built around the approved Lumi IP. The world should feel tactile, cinematic, and quietly magical: translucent silicone, subsurface light, glass memory fragments, shallow depth of field, and restrained volumetric illumination. The product is never explained. Its capabilities appear only as fleeting environmental evidence—a coordinate, a date, a photo-like prism, a mood color, and a path that remembers where Lumi has been.

## Character Lock

- Lumi keeps the approved one-piece rounded ghost/blob silhouette, two short feet, tiny side arms, two tall black oval eyes, and deep-violet location-pin accessory.
- Primary body color is a cool blue-to-lavender gradient with pearlescent translucency.
- Lumi has no mouth in the default state and is never replaced by particles, a generic orb, a robot, or a legacy mascot.
- Motion is weighty and elastic: floating, breathing, leaning, and looking—not flat translation.

## Colors

- Deep Space — `#070817` — cinematic void
- Midnight Violet — `#17133A` — spatial depth
- Lumi Blue — `#78A7FF` — key light and body energy
- Lumi Lavender — `#C3A7FF` — rim light and memory glow
- Pin Violet — `#4F38D6` — character accessory and path anchor
- Memory Warmth — `#FFCF8A` — remembered moment / photo prism
- Pearl Mist — `#F5F4FF` — final studio atmosphere
- Ink — `#141425` — final brand title

## Typography

- Chinese brand title: `PingFang SC`, weight 800, tightly tracked.
- Coordinates and dates: `Menlo`, weight 700, tabular figures, used only as environmental detail.
- No explanatory headlines, feature lists, launch language, or CTA.

## Motion

- Camera movement creates depth: foreground glass passes fastest, Lumi moves at mid-depth, background trails drift slowly.
- Scene 1 uses a slow camera orbit and breathing hover.
- Scene 2 uses a spatial iris through the memory prism, then a curved tracking move along the remembered path.
- Scene 3 uses a soft overexposure wipe into a calm studio portrait; the camera settles to stillness.
- Transitions are authored as scene reveals. Earlier scenes remain visually complete beneath the incoming scene.
- All loops are finite and deterministic.

## Product Easter Eggs

- A single coordinate readout suggests place without explaining maps.
- A glass fragment briefly catches a photo-like landscape.
- `09.27` appears for less than two seconds as a memory date.
- A small light bead shifts through cyan, gold, and pink as an emotional-state cue.
- A luminous path retains pins after Lumi passes.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use the retired WhatsDrink cover, green-hood mascot, robot, or any other legacy form.
- Do not replace the IP with a flat gradient blob, generic particle logo, or UI mockup.
- Do not show product screens, feature labels, onboarding copy, or dense text.
- Do not show `COMING SOON`, `微信小程序`, `即将上线`, download prompts, QR codes, or launch CTAs.
- Do not imitate Grok, OpenAI, or Kimi geometry, logos, timing, or assets.
- Do not use neon cyberpunk grids, hard glitches, or cheap looping icon carousels.
- Keep essential content clear of the bottom 360px and rightmost 160px platform-control zone.
